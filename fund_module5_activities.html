From Tools to a Strategy

⏱ About 12 minutes

Knowing the tools isn't a strategy. A funding strategy aligns the money you seek with your stage, your mission, and your tolerance for risk — and it's something you can present convincingly to funders. This final module brings the whole course together.

Three questions a funding strategy answers
1
Match to stage — is the venture at Blueprint, Validate, Prepare or Scale? Early stages favour own resources, friends & family, and grants; later stages open up loans, equity and social investment.
2
Match to mission — does this money keep the mission the principal beneficiary, or does it pull the venture off course? Mission-aligned money is worth more than it looks.
3
Assess the risk — can you meet repayment terms regardless of performance (debt)? Are you willing to share ownership (equity)? What happens if a funder pulls out?
💡 Sound financial planning means developing a clear financial roadmap, understanding risk, and ensuring long-term financial health — not just landing one grant. A credible plan is also what lets you pitch funding proposals convincingly.
🧭 For a venture you care about: name its stage, and the one funding source that best fits that stage right now.
