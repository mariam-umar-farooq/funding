The Social Enterprise Life Cycle

Social enterprises need funding at every stage — but which source fits depends on where they are. Not every venture moves through these in a straight line.

Source: "Where to get the money from?" slides by Assoc. Prof. Tsvetelina Marinova (life-cycle model: Kohl, 2012)
1

Blueprint

  • Understand customer needs
  • Develop the initial proposition
  • Business plan reflective of mission
  • Build core systems & prototypes
→
2

Validate

  • Conduct market trials
  • Test business-model assumptions
  • Refine model, systems, product
  • Is the mission intact?
→
3

Prepare

  • Stimulate demand & awareness
  • Strengthen supply chains
  • Build capacity to scale systems, talent & assets
→
4

Scale

  • New geographies & segments
  • Invest in talent & assets
  • Exploit systems & scale efficiencies
Early stages lean on own resources, friends & family, and grants · later stages open up loans, equity and social investment.
