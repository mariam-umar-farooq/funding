The Six Families of Funding

Financial mechanisms for social enterprises group into six broad families. Most real ventures use a mix, matched to their stage and mission.

Source: "Where to get the money from?" slides by Assoc. Prof. Tsvetelina Marinova
🐷

1 · Self-financing

Own capital and members' contributions (bootstrapping). Keeps full control, grows without debt.

No repayment
👨‍👩‍👧

2 · Friends & family

People who back the founder, often on lenient terms — but relationships are at stake.

Flexible
🎁

3 · Grants

Awards and donations from foundations, governments and donor funds — gifts not normally repaid.

No repayment
📦

4 · Surpluses & assets

Managing the firm's own resources well, and proceeds from selling long-term assets.

Own resources
🏦

5 · Lending / debt

Loans repaid with interest; concessional loans and crowd/P2P lending. Must be repaid regardless.

Repay + interest
📈

6 · Equity

Capital in exchange for a share of the enterprise; includes venture capital for proven, scaling ventures.

Shares ownership
Plus hybrids: social impact bonds and mezzanine finance blend features of debt and equity.
